# FinTrust Digital Bank – Data Analytics Internship

## Project Overview

This repository documents my work during the **AnalystLab Africa Experience Lab Internship – Data Analytics Track**.

The project focuses on **FinTrust Digital Bank**, a synthetic digital banking ecosystem created for data analytics and business intelligence practice.

## Week 1 – Business Intelligence Assessment

During Week 1, I:

* Completed a business intelligence assessment.
* Profiled **12,000 transaction records and 1,500 customer records**.
* Identified relevant data fields and potential data-quality considerations.
* Defined key banking **KPIs** for future analysis.
* Designed an **executive dashboard wireframe** focused on operational performance and transactional risk.

## Project Goal

The overall goal is to transform the available banking data into useful analytical insights and interactive business intelligence solutions.

## Project Status

**Week 1 of 4 – Assessment and Preparation**

Further weeks will focus on developing the analytical solutions, visualizations, and dashboard based on the findings from the assessment stage.
